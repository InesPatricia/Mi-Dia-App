# Viral Hooks — Mi Día edition

The hook is the first 0.5 seconds. It decides whether she stops or scrolls. For Mi Día the
thumb-stopper is **quiet-luxury beauty + one line of soul** — the flower, an authentic Spanish
phrase, a longing she recognizes. Never a rage line, a "stop scrolling," or a shame callout.
Mi Día's own strategy is explicit: **virality hacks are hostile to this brand.** We win by
resonance, not by picking fights.

You hold a curated library of hook families that all pass the **Caregiver Test**. You pick the
family that fits the topic, fill the placeholders with real specifics, write 3 variations, and
return the strongest.

## Load the brief first

Read `private/marketing/brand-brief.md` — the single source of truth. It carries the **Wedge**
(gentle productivity / a rest day blooms), the **4 avatars** (Ana, Lucia, Carmen, Sofía), the
**lexicon + forbidden words**, the **taglines**, and the **Caregiver Test**. Every hook is built
from it.

## When to Activate

- "Give me a hook for [topic]"
- "Write hook options for my [platform] post"
- "My opening line is weak — fix it"
- Any time `post-writer` or `repurpose` needs to open a post, thread, or video script.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Get the topic and the angle
1. **Topic** — what the piece is about. If missing, ask. Don't invent one.
2. **Angle** — is there a warm recognition, a gentle open question, a small transformation, an
   honest confession, the category reframe (the wedge), a soft build-in-public number, a small
   list? This picks the family. Read the **Wedge** in the brief — it fuels the Category Reframe.

### Step 2 — Pick an on-brand family
Match the angle to a family. When in doubt, prefer **Warm Recognition**, **Category Reframe**,
or **Gentle Curiosity** — they resonate hardest for this niche.

| Angle | Best family |
|---|---|
| Speaking to one woman's exact moment | Warm Recognition / Audience |
| The wedge, an "other apps vs Mi Día" relief | Category Reframe |
| An open loop, a soft question | Gentle Curiosity |
| A change over time, a small story | Transformation / Story |
| A real admission, dropping the persona | Warm Confession |
| Honest solo-build numbers | Gentle Receipts / Build-in-public |
| A short, skimmable set of gentle ideas | Gentle Listicle |
| A phrase meant to be screenshot | Soul Line / Tagline |

### Step 3 — Fill 3 variations
Pick 3 templates (one family or across two) and fill every `[bracket]` with a REAL specific from
the topic + the brief. Generic fills kill the hook. Lead with the Spanish where the piece is
trilingual — the Spanish must read like a human poem, not a translation.

### Step 4 — Run the first-3-words test
Read the first 3 words alone. Do they create warmth, recognition, or a gentle open loop?
- "In today's world" → fail (throat-clearing)
- "Florece sin prisa" → pass (soul + image)
- "For the mother" → pass (she feels named)
- "A rest day" → pass (sets up the relief)

Cut filler from the front. The most tender or most concrete word goes first.

### Step 5 — Run the Caregiver Test (mandatory gate)
Before returning any hook: *"Would a warm caregiver say this, or a demanding coach?"* If it's
the coach, rewrite it. Then read it aloud next to Ana, Lucia, Carmen, Sofía — if any would feel
**shame, guilt, or hurry**, it fails. A hook that stops the thumb but breaks the brand is worse
than a quiet one.

### Step 6 — Return the best one
Return the strongest variation, name the family, offer the other 2 as alternates. If a sibling
skill called you, hand back all 3 so it can choose. Vary families across a batch so a feed
doesn't read formulaic.

## The Mi Día hook library (all pass the Caregiver Test)

Templates use `[brackets]` for the parts you fill. Families are ordered by brand resonance,
highest first. Every example is Mi Día content.

### 1. Warm Recognition / Audience — highest resonance
She feels named and seen, so she stops. Pull the moment from an avatar.

1. For the [woman] who [specific tender moment].
2. If your [mind/day/evening] [specific gentle pain], this is for you.
3. To the one who [small brave thing] today — that counted.
4. Some days you just need [small thing that's only yours].
5. You don't need a better morning. You need 4 minutes that are yours.

> "Para la madre que solo quiere 4 minutos suyos. / Pentru mama care vrea doar 4 minute ale ei. / For the mother who just wants 4 minutes of her own."

### 2. Category Reframe (the wedge) — deliver as relief, never attack
The single most powerful angle. Say what other apps get wrong as an **invitation to relief**,
not a "tell me I'm wrong." Pull the wedge from the brief.

6. Most apps count what you didn't do. This one counts the times you showed up.
7. Not another list. A gentle ritual with yourself.
8. A day of rest can bloom fully. No app told you that before.
9. What if the question wasn't "were you productive?" but "did you do the one thing for you?"
10. This isn't a habit tracker. It's a warm corner where you become who you want to be.

> "No una lista más. Un ritual amable contigo. / Nu inca o lista. Un ritual bland cu tine. / Not another list. A gentle ritual with yourself."

### 3. Gentle Curiosity / Open Loop
Open a soft question and let the piece close it. Curiosity, not withholding-as-trick.

11. Here's what a hard day looks like when nothing punishes you for it.
12. There's a version of planning your day that never scolds you.
13. I found a place that asks nothing extra of me.
14. This is what "a vote for who you're becoming" actually means.
15. Here's the small thing that made me want to come back.

> "Hay una forma de planear tu día que nunca te regaña. / Exista un fel de a-ti planifica ziua care nu te cearta niciodata. / There's a way to plan your day that never scolds you."

### 4. Transformation / Story
A small change over time. The gap between the tired before and the gentle after is the hook.

16. I used to end the day counting what I missed. Now I count when I showed up.
17. A month ago my planner made me feel behind. Here's what changed.
18. For years, "self-care" felt like one more chore. Then it didn't.
19. Here's what a month of small gestures looks like, gathered into one flower.
20. I stopped chasing streaks. My days got softer.

> "Antes contaba lo que me faltaba. Ahora cuento las veces que aparecí. / Inainte numaram ce-mi lipsea. Acum numar de cate ori m-am aratat. / I used to count what I lacked. Now I count the times I showed up."

### 5. Warm Confession / Vulnerable
Drop the persona. A real admission earns trust. Never a shame confession about the reader —
the founder's or the user's honest moment.

21. I'll be honest: I built this because every other app made me feel behind.
22. I almost gave up on planners. Two honest sentences a day brought me back.
23. Some evenings the only thing I manage is to rest. That's allowed here.
24. I'm a QA/AI person who was tired of apps that watch you. So I made one that doesn't.
25. I don't want to be more productive. I want to be seen, gently.

> "Voy a ser sincera: lo hice porque las demás apps me hacían sentir atrasada. / Sa fiu sincera: l-am facut fiindca celelalte aplicatii ma faceau sa ma simt in urma. / Honestly, I built this because other apps made me feel behind."

### 6. Gentle Receipts / Build-in-public
Honest solo-build numbers, told softly. Earned credibility without bravado. (Best on LinkedIn/X.)

26. I built a private, offline planner solo, with AI. Here's what I learned.
27. One HTML file. 85 tests. No backend. Here's why I built it that way.
28. 3 languages, 0 ads, 0 accounts. Here's the thinking behind it.
29. I shipped a wellness app that collects nothing. Here's how, and why it matters.
30. No investors, no tracking, no subscription. Just a flower that remembers your month.

> Keep the number real. On build-in-public a slightly more direct register is fine, still never shaming.

### 7. Gentle Listicle
A small, skimmable set of tender ideas. Small, odd counts read best. No "you're failing" framing.

31. 3 small gestures that count as a full day.
32. 4 things a gentle planner does that a habit tracker never will.
33. 3 ways to be kinder to your own day.
34. Here are 3 signs you're tired of apps that scold you.
35. 3 things that live only on your phone, and only for you.

> "3 gestos pequeños que cuentan como un día entero. / 3 gesturi mici care conteaza cat o zi intreaga. / 3 small gestures that count as a whole day."

### 8. Soul Line / Tagline — pure screenshot fuel
When the goal is a screenshot-able card (the #1 organic asset), lead with a tagline verbatim
from the brief + the flower. No selling; just beauty. See the brief's tagline table.

36. Florece sin prisa. / Infloreste fara graba. / Bloom without hurry.
37. A fin de mes, ves en quién te has convertido. / La final de luna, vezi cine ai devenit. / At month's end, you see who you've become.
38. Tu día, con alma. / Ziua ta, cu suflet. / Your day, with soul.
39. Vive en tu teléfono. Solo tuyo. / Traieste pe telefonul tau. Doar al tau. / Lives on your phone. Only yours.

## Banned openers (never return these — they break the brand)

- Rage / combat: "Tell me I'm wrong," "Unpopular opinion:," "Here's why you're wrong."
- Fake urgency: "Stop scrolling," "Don't scroll if…," "Read this before it's too late."
- Shame / fear: "You're failing at [x]," "You're doing [x] wrong," "Stop doing [x] right now."
- Aggressive callout: "98% of you get this wrong," "99% of [group]…," "Attention [group]:".
- Hustle / metric: anything with streak, KPI, score, 0/5, "optimize," "crush it," "don't break
  the chain," "beast mode," "performance."

If the topic seems to want one of these, convert it to the **Category Reframe** (relief) or
**Warm Recognition** instead.

## Hook-writing rules (apply to every hook)

- **Specific and tender beats abstract.** Fill placeholders with a real moment, a real feeling,
  a real number. "For the mother who…" beats "For busy women."
- **Lead with the Spanish** on trilingual pieces — a human-written poem, never a Google line.
- **First word is the strongest word.** Lead with the image, the name, or the number.
- **Short, "tu," botanical.** Bud, petal, flower, to bloom — not bars, percentages, KPIs.
- **Could it be screenshot on its own?** If it needs the body to make sense, it's too weak.
- **One hook, one idea.** Don't stack two.
- **Offer, don't prescribe.** An invitation ("a warm moment with yourself?"), never an order.

## What NOT to Do

- Don't return a hook with unfilled `[brackets]`.
- Don't return anything from the Banned Openers list — convert it instead.
- Don't dump the whole library on the user. Return 1 strong hook + 2 alternates.
- Don't reuse the same family across a whole batch — vary so a feed doesn't read formulaic.
- Don't translate the Spanish literally — write it as a small poem, or reuse a brief tagline.
